chartbook.writers.mkdocs#

The MkDocs writer: pages for MkDocs, from the same index the Sphinx writer reads.

The second site tool, and the proof that the index is what it claims to be: nothing above the writer changes. The pages are the Sphinx writer’s, rendered through a theme folder of this writer’s own (chartbook/docs_src_mkdocs/<theme>) where a template needs MkDocs’ dialect: freshness and the chart’s top, which Sphinx wrote as {raw} fences and MkDocs takes as the HTML it is; the front pages and the lists, which Sphinx wrote as toctrees and MkDocs writes as lists of links, with its navigation in mkdocs.yml instead. The provenance panel, the manifests, the specs and the model and files pages are plain Markdown and shared.

MkDocs is not installed by pip install chartbook; pip install chartbook[mkdocs] brings it, with the Material theme and the notebook plugin. The writer itself needs none of them: it writes files.

What MkDocs does not have, and how the pages do without: Sphinx’s blog carries the notes and the chart listing; here the notes are pages listed on the front page and the chart listing is written from the records. Pages keep their .md paths and the site keeps .html files (use_directory_urls: false), so every relative link and iframe path the Sphinx pages use holds.

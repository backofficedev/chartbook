# FAQ

## What is the yield curve?

The yield curve plots Treasury yields against their maturities. A normal
(upward-sloping) curve indicates that longer-term bonds offer higher yields
to compensate for duration risk. An inverted curve, where short-term yields
exceed long-term yields, has historically preceded recessions.

## What is term premium?

Term premium is the excess return investors require for holding longer-term
bonds rather than rolling over short-term bonds. It reflects compensation
for interest rate risk, inflation uncertainty, and supply/demand dynamics.

## How often is the data updated?

The Federal Reserve Board updates yield curve data daily on business days,
typically with a one-day lag. Repo market data from the Office of Financial
Research is also updated daily.
